The change (patch_v155.py):
  TEMPLATE_GUARD_SECONDS = 7150.0. Once 7,150 s have elapsed since model loading started, predict_image returns,
  for every supplied box, a box-relative mean-pose template without any model forward: 17 joints at the mean
  normalised position (x/w, y/h) of the development boxes (template_pose.json, joint order of
  src/upar_pose/constants.py; the two ear joints use the nose position), visibility 1, person score 0.01. A non-finite box gives the template at (0, 0). The first use is printed
  once ("[submission] completion guard: mean-pose template for the remaining images after ... s (... images)").
  The guard comes after every other controller stage (tier walk, measured centre-padding fallback, member drops and
  the 7,120 s single-member emergency mode). Before 7,150 s the code path is identical to v154; if the run finishes
  before 7,150 s the predictions are those of v154.
Motivation: in a simulated host that was six times slower than the author's GPU (k=6), even the single-member
  centre mode was projected to exceed the documented 7,200 s limit (about 8,240 s for 30,245 images). The guard turns
  such a would-be time-limit kill into a completed run in which only the remaining images receive the template
  (template predictions are expected to score close to zero AP for those persons).
